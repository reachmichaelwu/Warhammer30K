How to fight

  • You command the Imperial Fists. Gold tokens are yours. Rust tokens are the Iron Warriors.
  • Click one of your units. Green hexes are legal moves. Red hexes are enemies you can attack.
  • Walls and chem-drains block movement. Vehicles cannot enter thorn scrub or bastions. Enemy units stop you in the adjacent hex.
  • A unit may move once, then attack once. Heavy and ordnance weapons strike less accurately after moving.
  • Curtain walls and thorn scrub block shooting. The road through the Iron Gate does not.
  • Stand on a strongpoint to take it. Empty strongpoints keep the last owner.
  • Win by destroying the Iron Warriors, by holding every strongpoint after their reply, or by holding two when turn 10 ends.
  • This is a simplified hex conversion of repository characteristics, not the Horus Heresy tabletop rules.
  • Import accepts panzerhammer-map-v1 or panzerhammer-scenario-v1 JSON. The built-in field is an original placeholder.
